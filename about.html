About

Enterprise AI Architect for Treasury

Arjun Krishnan, CTP

Founder & CEO, Valorean Technologies Inc.

arjun@valorean.ai

Treasury is entering its most significant transformation since the introduction of enterprise ERP systems. Enterprise AI now has the ability to reason across enterprise data, augment human judgment, and fundamentally reshape how treasury organizations operate, make decisions, and manage risk.

As an Enterprise AI Architect for Treasury, I work directly with CFOs and treasury leaders to define how Enterprise AI should be applied within treasury operations, workflows, and decision-making — and, just as importantly, where it should not. My focus is not technology for its own sake, but architecture that respects the controls, risk management, and professional judgment that define the treasury function.

Enterprise AI is evolving rapidly, and I see myself as a practitioner continually learning, listening, experimenting, and sharing what I learn with the treasury community. My aim is to contribute to the profession by exchanging ideas and helping treasury organizations navigate this new era with confidence and humility.

Before founding Valorean, I spent nearly three decades in enterprise treasury and finance, most recently as a Managing Director with EY, where I built and led the firm's SAP Treasury Practice in the United States. Throughout my career, I have advised many of the world's largest multinational organizations on treasury transformation, global SAP Treasury implementations, treasury centralization, payment modernization, and finance transformation.

Credentials & Publications

I am a Certified Treasury Professional (CTP) and a qualified accountant. I am a published author, speaker, and educator on enterprise treasury.

My published books:

  • Enterprise AI for Treasury: A Guide to Agentic Implementation (Krishbooks, 2026) — foreword by Brad Larson
  • Introducing SAP Treasury and Risk Management on S/4HANA (SAP Press, 2021)
  • SOX Compliance with SAP Treasury and Risk Management (SAP Press, 2009) — co-author

The mission

Treasury has always been defined by judgment, controls, and trust. Enterprise AI succeeds when it augments those qualities — not when it replaces them.

My mission is to help Treasury leaders explore how enterprise AI can enhance decision-making, strengthen governance, and create lasting business value — while preserving the controls, professional judgment, and trust that define the treasury function.

Through writing, speaking, research, and collaboration with treasury professionals around the world, I hope to contribute to the next chapter of our profession as we collectively explore what Enterprise AI makes possible.
